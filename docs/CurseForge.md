# Infinity Nexus: Satisfactory Build Tool

## 🏗️ Industrial Construction for Minecraft

**Satisfactory Build Tool** brings the precise, holographic building workflow of *Satisfactory* to Minecraft. Stop placing blocks one by one and start engineering your world.

### ✨ Key Features

#### 📐 Precision Holograms
*   **Preview First**: See exactly what you are building with a ghost hologram.
*   **Fix & Nudge**: Press **`X`** to lock the hologram in mid-air. Then use your **Arrow Keys** to nudge it into the perfect position. No more misclicks!

#### 🚀 Industrial Zooping
*   **Mass Construction**: Lock your hologram (`X`), toggle Zoop (`C`), and look in a direction.
*   **Instant Rows**: Place 10, 20, or more foundations in a single click. Essential for building large factory floors and bridges.

#### 🧨 Smart Dismantling
*   **Undo Button**: Switch to **Destruction Mode** (Red) and right-click any structure you placed to remove it entirely.
*   **Full Refund**: Get 100% of your resources back instantly. Experiment freely.
*   **Safety**: Only structures placed by this mod can be dismantled.

#### 🚜 Area Management
*   **Clear & Flatten**: Select two points (Shift+Right-Click) and choose to clear everything or flatten terrain.
*   **Block Replacement**: Hold a block in your off-hand while flattening to replace the base layer.

#### 🎨 Customizer & Materials
*   **Color Palettes**: Change the color of your factories on the fly using the built-in color grid.
*   **Material Swapping**: Change Foundations and Walls from Stone to **Iron, Gold, or Concrete**.
*   **Dynamic Holograms**: Previews update in real-time to show your selected color and material before you build.

---

### 🎮 How to Controls
*   **`M`**: Cycle Modes (Construction, Destruction, Clear).
*   **`B`**: Open Structure Library Menu.
*   **`Z`**: Cycle Structure Variant (same category).
*   **`R`**: Rotate Hologram (90° clockwise).
*   **`X`**: **Fix/Lock Position** (enables Nudge & Zoop).
*   **`C`**: **Toggle Zoop** (requires Fixed mode).
*   **`Arrows/PgUp/PgDn`**: **Nudge** (requires Fixed mode).
